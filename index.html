My reverence of steel

SIGNALS IN STEEL

By Malcolm Chris S. Orapa • October 2026

It listens and it speaks, it's interconnected to everything in my hands.

Cold, rigid, and inanimate. Yet it flows with data, I pass one everyday, and I say it's alive. After all, its blood is information.

Silent, wired, and tall.

I have no need to wait for daylight, these towers have captured and controlled lightning as its conduits. I praise them for their power, but I keep my head low to avoid its surges.

Cool icy relief, bringing the arctic to you.

No more hot blistering days or humid nights wil lever touch me again, I can ask for cold air at my request.

Our faithful guardians.

The gargoyles of old are no more, I have these machines protecting me with their unblinking gazes.

The tallest.

Appearing silently in day and night, these titans forged my home, and countless others, with their strength alone.

A break from steel

One of the littlest residents residing under the machines, hopeful but unaware of the relevance of steel. It's my friend.

A marvel.

A portion of a larger structure. Hand-crafted by machines and the human mind, standing tall and perfect.

The Giant in the horizon

The steel mountain, a colossus of engineering. I do not know what this giant holds within...

The first flames

Before the era of machines and giants, we had sparks and flames. Sparks that welded wire and steel together into the machines we have today.

The true signal.

Despite the greatness of machines and steel giants, they all adhere to the one above. The Sun. Steel must be strong so as not to melt under its heat. Some machines even feed on its heat to power themselves for us, that is the power of the true signal.
